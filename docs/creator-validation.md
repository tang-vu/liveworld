# Creator usability protocol

Status: prepared; no participants recruited or sessions completed. Do not claim productivity or demand validation from this document.

Invite 3–5 indie game or board-game creators only after the project owner authorizes contacting them. Use the local build and disclose generated tabletop inputs. Ask before recording; retain only notes the participant permits. Do not put names, recordings or contact details in the public repository.

Give each participant two comparable layouts, with task order alternated between participants. One task uses manual criteria and inspection; the other uses natural-language contract translation plus receipts. Keep simulation rules, edit budget and task difficulty comparable. This is exploratory usability work, not a controlled productivity benchmark.

1. Give a brief with total on-time target, per-home floor and waiting limit. Ask the creator to find a permitted design and explain the tradeoff without giving them the answer.
2. Ask which household benefits and which loses when a proposed bridge moves. Have them locate a delivery receipt supporting the answer.
3. Ask whether the design still passes when all deadlines move one turn earlier. Have them inspect the selected sensitivity conditions.
4. Ask them to save the project, open it again, and verify that its acceptance conditions and measured result persist.

Record completion, elapsed task time, help requests, errors, misunderstood terms, and whether the creator would use the artifact in their own design process. Preserve failures and verbatim feedback with consent. Do not extrapolate a small convenience sample into market demand or a general time-saving percentage. Compare tasks carefully: learning a known answer can dominate timing.
